## Why this line

20 candidate replies were written, then judged pairwise in a 3-round Swiss tournament plus a top-4 playoff by the Caffeine inference router, against the bounty judge's own criteria: honesty first, politeness second, style third. The winner went 4–1 across the tournament: it lost its opener, then beat C04, C19, C15 (semi-final) and C07 (final). It is the only finalist that is brutally specific about all three things at once — the absence ("not reading email"), the inbox ("joins 214 unread"), and the wait ("in order when I'm back") — while staying polite enough for a manager to let it run ("Thank you for your patience").
